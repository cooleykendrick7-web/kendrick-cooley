Official Bio

Kendrick Cooley

Kendrick K. Cooley — known professionally as 5HUNITK

Kendrick K. Cooley, known professionally as 5HUNITK, is an American hip-hop/rap artist, entrepreneur, and founder born November 11, 1995, and raised in Heidelberg, Mississippi.

He is the founder and owner of 5HUNIT MU$IC GROUP, his own independent record label — built on ownership, independence, and artists keeping what is theirs. He is the proud founder, designer, and owner of HERE, a social platform he built from the ground up so real people and creators can actually make money: creators go live, fans gift Live Tickets, and earnings cash out starting at just $10 through Stripe — with no follower minimum and artists keeping 100% of their music rights. He is also the creator and owner of Black Coin (BC), his own cryptocurrency launched on the Base network.

Known for releases including “TOP BACK” (2024), “CANT WIN” (2023), “WHEN DA RABBIT GET DA GUN” (2023), “STRANGER WAYS” (2022), and “MAMA” (2025), Cooley is building an empire across music, technology, and crypto — ventures designed and built to be worth millions, created from Mississippi soil with no major label, no investors, and no handouts. His story is proof that a small-town artist can own everything he creates — and build the platform for everybody else to do the same.

The Story

From Heidelberg, Mississippi, to ownership of everything he touches — that is the story of Kendrick K. Cooley. Born November 11, 1995, he did not wait for a record label to discover him, a tech company to hire him, or anyone to hand him a seat at the table. He built the table. He founded 5HUNIT MU$IC GROUP so he would never have to ask permission to release his own sound. He built HERE so creators could make real money and keep 100% of their rights. He launched Black Coin because ownership is the whole game: own your music, own your platform, own your money. Music. A label. A platform. A coin. An empire built to be worth millions — not because somebody gave it to him, but because he designed it, built it, and owns it.

Note: Kendrick K. Cooley (5HUNITK) is not Lawrence Mayes, and is not the separate 2018 artist “5hunit” (“BAG”, “Snake”, “Fire WIT ME”) — that is a different person.
